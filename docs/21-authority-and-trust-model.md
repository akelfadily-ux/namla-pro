# 21 · Authority and trust model

## EffectiveAuthority

`EffectiveAuthority = HardSecurityPolicy ∩ HumanOrBuildLawAuthorization ∩ TrustedKernelPermit ∩ PlanContractCapabilityScope ∩ RuntimeBudget ∩ EnvironmentCapability`

Authority is deny-by-intersection. Any denying term blocks the effect.

No agent, provider, Plan, Protocol, Pro, Colony, Son, or Leggo may mint authority by writing capability names into data.

## Trust taxonomy

- providers/agents: untrusted cognition
- PlanContract: requirements/scope, not authority
- Trusted Kernel: authority and mechanical observation
- Assessments: semantic interpretation, not mechanical fact
- GateVerdict: gate-local decision under versioned policy
- HumanDecisionRecord: explicit scoped authority/judgment

## Forgery resistance

Serializable objects that merely look like permits do not constitute authority. Real permit/approval schemes must preserve the strongest existing unforgeability and scope-binding guarantees discovered during Rescue Census.
